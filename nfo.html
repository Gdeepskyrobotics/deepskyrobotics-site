SECURITY

Your controllers can't be reached, so they can't be attacked

Because NFO is 100% on-premises, there is no cloud tenant, no vendor tunnel, and no outbound AI service touching your plant. The reasoning runs beside your PLCs, not between them and the internet — so malicious-AI attacks on your controllers have nothing to grab onto.

No path in

All data, models and reasoning stay inside the building. Air-gap it entirely — NFO never needs the internet to work.

Read-only by design — for now

NFO listens to Modbus and OPC UA; it never writes to the PLC. The AI can recommend — only your people can actuate. Supervised write-back is on the roadmap, opt-in and gated the same way.

Auditable, not autonomous

Every finding and recommendation is persisted locally with its evidence, so you can verify exactly what the AI saw and said.
