Everything we're building between the floor and the work order

Four ways in

Built: read-only Modbus TCP, OPC UA and EtherNet/IP connectors, plus replay for commissioning. Tag maps live in plain YAML, with the gateway switched by a single setting. Tested on simulators only.

modbus_tags.yaml · opcua_nodes.yaml

Four reasoning engines

Built, demo on simulated data: threshold, missing-signal, flat-line, spike and slow-drift checks; ML anomaly scoring (shadow-only); cross-tag correlation, reported as correlation, not cause; episode lifecycle. A drift flag is not a failure forecast.

Sensor · SensorML · Correlation · Episode

Episodes, not alert storms

Built, demo on simulated data: an ongoing condition is one persistent episode with per-episode emit cooldown, and stale episodes auto-close. Severity is meant to be unambiguous: info, warning, critical.

open → tracked → closed · full history kept

Datasheet-grounded AI

Built, demo on simulated data: upload a manufacturer datasheet and limits are extracted from it. The aim is layout-aware extraction of real spec tables, so recommendations cite rated limits, maintenance intervals and model ids. A rated interval is quoted as the manufacturer's figure, never as a predicted replacement date.

PDF · TXT · MD · CSV · JSON — bound to asset + tag

Desktop-grade deployment

Built and released as v2.0.0 and v2.1.0: a per-user Windows installer with no admin prompt, system tray, single-instance lock, and checksum-verified auto-updates. Unsigned, and never installed at a plant.

built (unsigned): v2.0.0 · v2.1.0 · planned: NFO-Setup.exe · runtimes bundled · no Python

Designed for uptime

Built, demo on simulated data: a write-ahead-logged SQLite local store, indexes and a TTL cache. A single batch endpoint is aimed at returning the whole dashboard in under 3 ms — a target, not a measurement.

SQLite WAL · TTL cache · /dashboard target: under 3 ms

SECURITY

Designed to keep your controllers out of reach

NFO is being designed to run entirely on-premises, with no cloud tenant, no vendor tunnel, and no outbound AI service touching your plant. The reasoning is planned to run beside your PLCs, not between them and the internet — the aim being to leave malicious-AI attacks on your controllers little to grab onto.

Air-gapped as the launch goal

The goal is for all data, models and reasoning to stay inside the building. The initial release is planned to be fully air-gapped — NFO is meant to install and run with no internet connection at all.

Read-only toward the plant

NFO's connector code contains no write calls: it listens to Modbus, OPC UA and EtherNet/IP and never writes to a PLC, a CMMS or equipment (built; tested on simulators only). NFO advises; your people decide and act, with lockout/tagout before any hands-on work.

Auditable by design, not autonomous

Findings and episodes are persisted locally with their evidence (built; demo on simulated data), so you can verify what the AI saw and said.
