THE ENGINE

The only engine you'll ever need

NEPHES is a reasoning engine for signals that matter. Machines and markets speak in continuous streams of numbers — NEPHES listens, understands what's normal, notices what isn't, and says why, with the evidence attached. And because it adapts to the latest and greatest in AI infrastructure on its own, you never have to chase it. It's the core every Deep Sky Robotics product is built on.

HOW IT THINKS

Observe → Reason → Judge → Explain

01 · OBSERVE

Any stream, one language

Sensor telemetry, market ticks, token flows — every source is normalized into one canonical stream the engine can reason over, in real time.

02 · REASON

A fabric of specialists

Instead of one monolithic model, NEPHES routes each signal through specialized reasoning engines — limits, patterns, anomalies, cross-signal relationships — that each look for something different.

03 · JUDGE

Confidence before action

Every conclusion carries a calibrated confidence. Below the bar, NEPHES keeps watching instead of acting — knowing when not to fire is the feature.

04 · EXPLAIN

Evidence, not verdicts

Outputs arrive as findings with their reasoning trail — what was observed, what it was compared against, why it matters — and persist as an auditable history.
